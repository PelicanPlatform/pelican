# Managing Data Access

Pelican decides who may read and write the objects your origin serves.
This section is about controlling that: creating a **collection** over part of your storage, deciding which groups can read or write it, and handing day-to-day control to the people who own the data.

It does not cover exporting storage in the first place.
A collection controls access *inside* a namespace your origin already exports; see [Federating Your Data](/sharing-your-data/federating-your-data) to create the export.

## The shape of the job

Granting access takes three pieces, and they always fit together the same way:

1. **A collection** marks the prefix you want to control — a dataset, a project directory, a research group's storage area.
2. **A group** is who you grant to. Access is never granted to a person directly; you put people in a group and grant the group.
3. **A grant** ties the two together, giving that group `read` or `write` on the collection.

Once that is set up, the collection's owner and its admin group run it without needing any server-wide privilege — and the people they grant access to can pass a subset of it on to their own collaborators as a **share**.
